David Richter

Hi!

I am interested in programming language theory and practice, and correct and efficient compilers for domain-specific languages using functional programming and strong types. Usually, I write Lean proofs or Scala programs.

You can find my email at my university page, or in any of my published papers.

Research Projects#

My work develops functional domain-specific languages together with their compilers, and proves the guarantees that matter (preserved semantics and parallelism, deadlock freedom, sound incremental updates), mostly mechanized in Lean or Coq. The four projects below apply this approach to different domains. See Publications for the full list, and the CV for courses and supervised theses.

Choreographic Programming Write a whole distributed system as one script, and let the compiler split it up. Array & Differentiable Programming Compilers for machine learning: derive and prove what ML frameworks hard-code. Incremental & Reactive Programming Compilers that turn plain functional code into computations that redo only what changed. Effects & Type Theory Notations and type checkers that let programmers write what they mean, and still check it.
